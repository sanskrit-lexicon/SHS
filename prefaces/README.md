# SHS front matter (prefaces)

Faithful OCR + translation of the **front matter** of the *Shabda-Sagara, or A Comprehensive Sanskrit-English Lexicon* (SHS) — Pandit-Kulapati Jībānanda Vidyāsāgara, **First Edition, Calcutta 1900** (Calcutta Press, No. 8 Bowbazar Street; printed by Mookerjee & Co.). The dictionary is "chiefly based on Professor Horace Hayman Wilson's Sanskrit-English Dictionary."

Source scans (Cologne Digital Sanskrit Dictionaries):
<https://sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/csldev/csldoc/build/dictionaries/prefaces/shspref.html>

**Source language: English** — so the base per-page `.md` files are already English (no separate `.en.md`); a Russian translation `.ru.md` is provided for every page. Digitizer running-header/footer stamps are omitted (they are not part of the original).

## Notes

- The title page gives the imprint, editions, and the booksellers (Ashu Bodha & Nitya Bodha Bhattacharyya); a binding tear slightly obscures "College" in the final imprint line.
- The Abbreviations page has two parts: an English **List of Abbreviations** (two columns) and a Devanāgarī **साङ्केतिकाः शब्दाः** table pairing सङ्केतः (abbreviation) with सङ्केतबोधः (full sense — the ten verbal *gaṇa*s plus voice/transitivity/derivation tags). In that Devanāgarī table the left-hand abbreviation column falls in the binding margin and is largely cut off in the scan; those cells, and a few faint/torn glyphs, are marked `[?]`.
